# Strategy Rules — Spot Swing (1h entry / 4h context)

Only TWO setups are tradeable. Anything else is `none`.

## Setup A — Trend Pullback

Context (4h): price above EMA200, EMA50 above EMA200, structure of higher highs/higher lows.

Entry conditions (1h), ALL required:
- Price pulled back to the EMA50 zone or a prior breakout level (support retest)
- RSI(14) dipped below 45 and is turning up (current value > previous value)
- A bullish reversal candle closed: hammer / engulfing / strong close in top 40% of range
- Pullback volume was lower than the preceding impulse volume (healthy retracement)

## Setup B — Range Breakout with Retest

Context (4h): price compressed in a range ≥ 30 bars (1h) with clearly defined high.

Entry conditions (1h), ALL required:
- Close above range high with volume ≥ 1.5× the 20-bar average
- EITHER currently retesting the broken level (preferred entry) OR within 0.5×ATR(14)
  of the breakout close
- BTC 4h is not in a strong downtrend
- No higher-timeframe resistance within 1R of entry

## Market regimes

- **Trend** (4h EMA50/200 aligned, ADX-like expansion, clean swings): Setup A allowed.
- **Range** (flat EMAs, price oscillating between defined levels): Setup B allowed
  only on confirmed breakout; buying mid-range is forbidden.
- **High-volatility chop** (huge wicks, ATR spiking, no structure): NOTHING is allowed.
  Reject everything and say so.
